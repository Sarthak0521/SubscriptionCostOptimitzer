## StreamNova Video Plans and Prices
StreamNova is a sample video streaming service with three monthly plans.
The Basic plan costs ₹199 per month and allows 1 screen at 720p resolution.
The Standard plan costs ₹499 per month and allows 2 screens at 1080p resolution.
The Premium plan costs ₹699 per month and allows 4 screens at 4K resolution.
All StreamNova plans include ads-free viewing and offline downloads on mobile.

## StreamNova Annual Billing and Refund Policy
StreamNova offers annual billing on the Standard and Premium plans only.
Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount compared with paying monthly for 12 months.
Annual subscribers may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase.
After 7 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable, but the plan stays active until the end of the paid year.
Monthly StreamNova plans are never refunded for partial months.

## StreamNova Cancellation Rules
A StreamNova monthly subscription can be cancelled at any time from the account settings page.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
There is no cancellation fee for StreamNova plans.
Downloaded videos stop playing once the subscription ends.

## CloudVault Storage Plans and Prices
CloudVault is a sample cloud storage service with two monthly plans.
The Lite plan costs ₹130 per month and provides 100 GB of storage for one user.
The Family plan costs ₹650 per month and provides 2 TB of storage shared by up to 5 users.
Both CloudVault plans include automatic photo backup.

## CloudVault Cancellation and Data Retention
CloudVault requires 30 days of notice to cancel a plan.
Users are billed for the notice period even if they stop using the service.
After cancellation, stored files are kept in read-only mode for 60 days and then permanently deleted.
Users who exceed their storage limit cannot upload new files until they free space or upgrade.

## FitPulse Fitness App Plans and Prices
FitPulse is a sample fitness app with a free tier and one paid plan.
The Free tier includes basic workout tracking with ads.
The Pro plan costs ₹299 per month and adds personalised training plans and no ads.
FitPulse Pro offers a 14-day free trial for first-time users.
If the trial is not cancelled before day 14, the first monthly charge of ₹299 is applied automatically.

## FitPulse Plan Limits and Device Support
FitPulse Pro can be used on up to 3 devices at the same time.
The Free tier can be used on 1 device only.
FitPulse syncs workout data with smartwatches on the Pro plan only.
FitPulse does not offer an annual billing option.

## General Price Change Policy
All sample providers in this catalogue must notify subscribers at least 30 days before a price increase.
A price increase applies from the next billing cycle after the notice period ends.
Subscribers who do not accept a price increase may cancel before it takes effect without any penalty.

## Plan Upgrade and Downgrade Rules
Upgrading to a more expensive plan takes effect immediately, and the price difference for the remaining days is charged on a pro-rata basis.
Downgrading to a cheaper plan takes effect from the next billing cycle.
No refund is given for the unused part of the higher plan when downgrading.

## Payment Methods and Failed Payments
All sample providers accept UPI, debit cards and credit cards.
If an automatic payment fails, the provider retries the payment after 3 days.
If the payment still fails after 2 retries, the subscription is paused until the payment is completed.
Paused subscriptions do not count as cancelled and can be resumed by paying the due amount.
